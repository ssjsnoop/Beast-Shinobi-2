# Beast Shinobi 2 art
Preserves the original painted heroes, wolves, Kitsune, enemies and six realm backdrops.
New original generated painted assets: black/gold armored, silver-haired Emperor Raizen with crimson cape and horned crown; dedicated undead rival and samurai sheets; atmospheric imperial palace under an eclipsed moon. Generated sheets were individually registered to consistent body/feet anchors. Emperor spacing/alpha were revised after visual inspection. Sprites are lossless WebP; backgrounds use high-quality WebP compression.
Emperor attack/cast/hurt/death frames support his timed boss moves. The new palace/title use the same new illustration, with a Canvas foreground of lacquer pillars, gilded walkway edges and banners. Title and true-ending typography/layout are responsive HTML/CSS. Prologue teleport and spirit extraction use live sprites and Canvas effects. Original inline art remains available as recovery when files fail to load.

Stormfire v3 replaces only the requested Dragon external sheet with a more imposing original painted sapphire/gold horned dragon. All 33 poses were measured independently; casting frames contain no baked projectiles. Authored mouth coordinates are transformed with the same per-pose anchors and scale as sprite drawing. Wolf mouth coordinates follow their existing approved art. Electric Wolf breath is gold-white; Dragon lightning is a thicker blue-white discharge. Frost and flame use distinct cones, shards/embers and charge effects. Original inline Dragon recovery art is retained.

Improvement Pass 1: approved sprite artwork retained. Extended elemental talons and the Dragon’s summoned fire hand are animated Canvas effects tied to actual combat reach. Three MP3 score loops are original compositions synthesized for this game with plucked-string, breath-flute, wood-percussion, bell and taiko-style timbres. No third-party music recordings are included. Sword and element sound effects use layered Web Audio synthesis.
